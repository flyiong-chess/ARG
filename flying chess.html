Aeroplane Chess

How to play

Two-player How to play: First, you need one or more dice. The two players play rock-paper-scissors, and the winner rolls the dice first. If you want it to be easier, rolling a 1, 2, 4, or 6 lets you get to the helipad. If you want it to be harder, you need to roll a 6 to get to the helipad. After that, whatever number you roll, your piece moves that many steps. There will be many colored floor tiles on the map. If you stop exactly on a tile with the same color as your piece, you can teleport to the next tile with the same color as your piece. If you run into your opponent's piece and stop exactly on it, you can capture it and make it start over from the beginning. There will be four shortcut routes on the map. When you reach the shortcut route that matches the color of your piece, you can go to the endpoint of that shortcut route. When you are 5 spaces away from the finish line, you must roll exactly the same number as the distance between you and the finish line. If it is not exact, the extra points will make you move backward. When you roll the exact number, you win. In the next round, the winner of the previous round rolls the dice first.
